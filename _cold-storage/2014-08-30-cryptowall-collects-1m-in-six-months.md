The family of file-encrypting ransomware known as [CryptoWall](http://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/malvertising-and-cryptowall-mark-the-appearance/) is proliferating quickly, with more than $1 million paid in ransoms to date. Though it became well-known in the first quarter of 2014, researchers believe that the malware is now accelerating to become the largest and most destructive ransomware threat on the internet.

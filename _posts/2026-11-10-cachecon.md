## CacheCon Infosec conference

A new privacy and hacker-forward infosec conference is coming to Fort Collins. CacheCon is a deliberately informal, hands-on event built for people who actually take things apart and put them back together—focusing on exploits, hardware, lockpicking, and practical counter-surveillance. Stripping away the corporate fluff, the organizers enforce a strict "no bullshit" ethos: no badge scans, no pay-to-speak vendor pitches, and no lead generation lists. If you're looking for an authentic gathering where showing the work matters more than slide decks, keep an eye on cachecon.com for updates and ways to get involved.
